As AI agents increasingly access critical infrastructure resources, traditional identity and access management approaches prove inadequate for autonomous workloads. HashiCorp Boundary addresses these challenges through dynamic credential management, just-in-time access provisioning, and comprehensive session auditing, with Vault playing a supporting role in managing the secrets and credentials that agentic AI systems require. The post demonstrates how HashiCorp's security products work together to maintain least-privilege access at scale as organizations adopt agentic AI architectures.
